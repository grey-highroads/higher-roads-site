Visual experiences for physical spaces.

Higher Roads is an art and technology studio in Nashville. We work across rooms, stages, venues, screens, tables, installations, and live environments.

See the work

The starting point

The room changes the job.

A stage is different from a table. A gallery is different from a venue. The same visual idea behaves differently in each one.

We start with the space, what people are doing there, and what the experience needs to accomplish. The format follows from there.

Selected work

Built in real rooms.

Mycopop launch environment

A five-day build for a functional beverage launch in Nashville. Projection across three walls, a playable branded game, panel visuals, and motion content turned the venue into part of the launch.

How we work

Start with what should happen.

The place

What is the room? Where are people standing? What already exists? What has to stay out of the way?

The behavior

What should people see or do? What changes during the experience? What does an operator need to control?

The build

We make the visual work and the technical pieces needed to make it happen, then test them in the space.

The Lab

Some projects begin with us.

Rabbit Ears is an analog-TV-style venue experience with original channels running from a small local media box. Other experiments explore projection, physical interfaces, sensing, and tools we want to exist before anyone asks for them.

For partners

A specialist layer when the brief needs one.

Agencies, fabricators, event producers, creative teams, and venues bring us in when a project crosses into projection, interaction, screens, or live media.

We can help shape the idea early or take a defined scope and build our part. White-label work is fine.
